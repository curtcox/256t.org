A hash function is a recipe that takes any amount of data, from nothing at all to an enormous file, and produces an output of a fixed size. For SHA five-twelve, that's always five hundred and twelve bits. It's deterministic: the same input always gives exactly the same output, on any computer, forever. And it's fast. Your laptop can hash hundreds of megabytes a second.

The usual metaphor is a fingerprint. It's a small thing that stands in for a big thing, and you can compare two fingerprints much more easily than you can compare two whole people.

But metaphors leak, and this one leaks in an important place. So let's find the leak.
